# Audio direction and feedback

## Design the listening hierarchy
Identify essential gameplay cues, ambience, music and speech. Choose spatial versus non-spatial playback and priorities deliberately. Preserve recognizable supplied item sounds when requested; do not fabricate provenance for downloaded samples.

Align sound onset/tail with actual actions and animation timing. Include variation and repetition control without masking the important cue. Define buses/settings and interruption behavior. A volume slider must alter the relevant channels rather than merely move visually.

Track licences/voice permission, creator ownership, upload moderation and runtime access separately. Previews use actual files; an asset ID alone is not proof it plays for the target experience. Optional generation has its own authorized provider and cost.

## Verification
Check clipping, silence, duration, repeated triggers, spatial falloff, late loading and observer/owner playback. Test overlapping action/voice/ambience and sound alternatives for critical information. Do not reuse Hunted broadcast rules for an unrelated music or social game.
