# Characters and animation-ready topology

## Fit the intended rig and style
Determine avatar/character family, proportions, movement needs and target deformation path before detailed sculpting. Separate NPCs, player avatars and marketplace wearables; their requirements and publication routes differ.

Block out silhouette, facial readability, costume and joints. Place topology where deformation needs it, not merely where it looks regular in a still image. Review elbows/shoulders/hips, attachments and clothing layers at action poses. Use retopology and texture baking where appropriate to the chosen quality/budget.

Keep stable names, rest pose, scale and material boundaries. Do not presume an arbitrary mesh can behave like a standard avatar or that a stock skeleton fits a custom creature. Rights for references and character likeness matter.

## Verification
Inspect neutral and extreme representative poses, skinning weights, penetration and small-screen readability. Validate the actual Roblox rig/import path and intended animation playback. Report unsupported skinned-mesh or avatar behavior explicitly. Never auto-publish a wearable simply because an NPC model preview passed.
