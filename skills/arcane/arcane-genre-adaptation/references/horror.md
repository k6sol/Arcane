# Horror / survival

The player decides when to expose themselves, conserve a scarce resource, investigate or help someone. Choose the intended fear and audience; suspense, pursuit, mystery and survival need different mechanics. Darkness and a jump-scare timer are not a complete design.

## From a slice to a journey

First prove a readable threat, objective, alternate route and fair retry. For a full brief, connect discoveries to changing objectives, new spaces/pressure and a resolution. Different chapters/areas should develop information or player decisions, not reset the same fuse hunt. Map escape loops, hiding credibility, resource sources/sinks and safe recovery before decoration. Do not impose a universal chapter count or stalker on a deliberately quiet mystery.

Threat perception, catches, pickups and objective progression are server-owned. Define what was seen/heard, how long it is remembered, how the threat disengages and how players can learn the rule. Cooperative item/door states and downed/spectator behavior need explicit ownership. Clean delayed scares, audio/camera changes, hiding anchors and path tasks on death, escape or scene change.

## Craft and acceptance

Use visibility contrast, useful landmarks, restrained positional cues, authored reveals and real idle/chase/reaction animation. Essential clues need legible non-audio alternatives; do not make stingers or flashes the only information. Match props/materials to the chosen setting and use actual authorized media rather than invented IDs. Read `arcane-horror-kit` for integration, `arcane-npc` for perception and audio/accessibility skills for presentation.

Observe players finding the next goal without coaching, recognizing danger, choosing different risks and recovering from failure. Test blocked routes, a seen hiding entry, repeated triggers, shared objectives and a complete ending/retry. Review actual light/cue readability on target displays. A scare-frame assertion or atmospheric render is not evidence of suspense, fair pursuit or a whole finished game.
