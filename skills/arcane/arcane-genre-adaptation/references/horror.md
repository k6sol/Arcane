# Horror / survival

## Player decision
Decide when to expose yourself, conserve resources or help another player.

## Craft priorities
Readable danger and escape counterplay; atmospheric pacing without constant visual noise.

## Representative vertical slice
One threat, one objective, two routes and a quick failure/retry loop.

## Player and technical checks
Observe whether players recognize threats and make different risk choices; check visibility on small displays.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
