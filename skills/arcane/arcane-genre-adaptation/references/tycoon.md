# Tycoon / builder

The player allocates limited resources between capacity, efficiency, layout or another production path. The business fantasy should be visible in operation. A line of price pads whose only effect is a larger income multiplier provides little strategic choice.

## Plan a functioning enterprise

First prove claim -> produce -> collect -> informed purchase -> visible change. For a full game, map the requested production branches, construction milestones and built-out/continued-play state. Later content can add bottlenecks, routing, service quality or new products rather than repeat the first dropper. Rebirth is optional and must explain what resets/survives; do not use it to hide absent content.

Keep plot ownership, purchase graph, production scheduler, profile and presentation separate. Server definitions determine prices, dependencies and credit. Simultaneous claims, duplicate collection and stale drops from a previous owner must not pay or modify the next plot. Persistent purchases require genuine requested durable grants; failed loads cannot spawn a default business and overwrite progress. Reference numbers in `arcane-tycoon-kit` are starting points, not player pacing evidence.

## Legible operation and acceptance

Lay out input, machinery/service, output and player circulation so cause/effect can be followed. Use original themed assets at coherent scale; animate working parts and pair useful sounds with throughput, purchase and refusal. HUD and world signage show actual price, benefit, prerequisites and balance. Empty/locked/loading states should explain the next action, not masquerade as broken buttons.

Test a whole production branch, alternative useful upgrade, insufficient funds, another owner's purchase, duplicate touch, leave/reclaim and requested save/rejoin. Measure intended fully developed plot load and transient drops/effects, not only the free starter. Observe whether a player can explain the trade-off between two upgrades and see its effect. A saved balance or decorative completed building alone does not prove an enjoyable, recoverable tycoon.
