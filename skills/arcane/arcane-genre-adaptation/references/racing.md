# Racing / vehicle skill

## Player decision
Trade speed against grip, route, braking or passing opportunity.

## Craft priorities
Stable vehicle behavior, readable track flow, fair starts and recoverable crashes.

## Representative vertical slice
One short track, one vehicle, a timing/result flow and two route choices.

## Player and technical checks
Measure handling consistency; test collision, ownership transfer and reset without rewarding exploits.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
