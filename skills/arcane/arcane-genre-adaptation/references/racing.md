# Racing / vehicle skill

The player trades speed against grip, braking, route and passing opportunity. Match the actual vehicle/camera/style before tuning; kart circuits, rally, time trials and flight races do not share one universal handling preset.

## The first lap is a milestone

Prove one vehicle on a short course containing representative steering, braking, transitions and recovery. For a full request, expand the promised course/event/vehicle roster with genuinely different decisions: technical corners, elevation, surface behavior, alternate lines or competitive pressure. Do not label a single starter circuit the finished game, or invent a mandatory map count for a small handling fix.

Keep race phases, eligible entrant/vehicle, ordered gates, server timing and result awards coherent. Walking through gates tests plumbing, not legitimate vehicle traversal. Wrong-way/skip/reset abuse, countdown starts, disconnect, DNF and late join behavior need explicit rules. Track/rules IDs qualify best times. Constraints and network ownership must be verified with actual driving, not assumed from sample torque numbers.

## Readable speed and acceptance

Landmarks, road edge contrast, sightlines and restrained surface effects help players plan a line. Engine/skid cues, steering/wheel animation, chase camera and speed/time/lap HUD follow actual state. Results explain performance and lead cleanly to retry/selection. Use original appropriate vehicles and scenery, no Toolbox by default, and real authorized audio/animation assets. See `arcane-racing-kit` plus physics, input-camera and gameplay-feel guidance.

Drive each delivered course and test corners, seams, crashes, reverse gates, resets and repeated races. Measure low-frame-rate control, collision/ownership transfer and multi-car behavior where available. Observe whether different routes/vehicles offer understandable trade-offs. Attribute assertions and nonuniform preview images do not prove handling, map variety, multiplayer fairness or native-engine asset fidelity; preserve those distinctions in the handoff.
