# Strategy / tactical management

## Player decision
Allocate scarce resources, anticipate another actor and commit to a plan.

## Craft priorities
Understandable information, readable counters and decisive rather than busywork actions.

## Representative vertical slice
One bounded encounter with a small set of options and two viable approaches.

## Player and technical checks
Inspect dominant strategies, information overload and consequences visible to new players.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
