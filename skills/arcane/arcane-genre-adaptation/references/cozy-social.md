# Cozy / social creation

The player expresses a preference, makes or places something and chooses how to interact with others. Offer self-directed goals and an inviting world; do not impose combat, a timer or an XP treadmill on a brief about relaxation and creativity.

## Useful activities and places

First prove one creation/customization activity, a safe interaction and recovery/rejoin if requested. A full experience needs the promised range of useful places, objects and social activities connected by approachable controls. A decorated empty plaza is not a creation game, and many color variants do not replace different uses. Give gardens, homes, shops or communal spaces a purpose consistent with the chosen fantasy.

Separate definitions, owned creations, placement state and sharing permissions. Preview -> valid place -> edit/undo -> save/share must use actual ownership and bounds. Respect another player's authored children and private space. Public publication, gifts and trading need their own consent/durable paths; a local success animation cannot mint shared ownership. Requested saved creations require conflict/migration handling and protected failed loads, not silently overwritten defaults.

## Craft without friction

Use coherent scale, material/color families, readable object previews and actual variant icons. Snapping, camera framing and gentle placement feedback should assist expression without making touch/controller users fight precision. Include cancel/undo, unavailable placement and storage-full states. Use optional goals and quiet animation/audio/VFX that can be reduced, not constant reward interruptions. Only actual authorized media and supported custom authoring/import capabilities count as delivered content.

Observe players selecting their own goals and completing the actual create/place/edit/share loop without coaching. Test private/public visibility, another user's denied edit, blocked invitations, deletion/undo, disconnect and requested rejoin. Review narrow-screen focus and large creation performance. `arcane-social-moderation`, inventory/interface/world-building and persistence guidance supply relevant mechanics. A pleasant screenshot or tutorial flag does not prove safe social play, creative breadth or enjoyment.
