# Cozy / social creation

## Player decision
Express a preference, create/place something and interact safely with other players.

## Craft priorities
Approachable controls, coherent inviting art, optional goals and low-friction sharing.

## Representative vertical slice
One creation/customization activity plus one safe social interaction and rejoin.

## Player and technical checks
Observe clarity and self-directed goals; test private/public visibility and device usability.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
