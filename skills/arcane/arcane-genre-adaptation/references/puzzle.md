# Puzzle / educational challenge

## Player decision
Form a hypothesis, manipulate a rule and use feedback to revise it.

## Craft priorities
Logical consistency, readable state, satisfying discovery and accessible input.

## Representative vertical slice
One introduced rule followed by a combination puzzle and reset.

## Player and technical checks
Test alternative valid solutions, deadlocks and whether feedback teaches without revealing everything.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
