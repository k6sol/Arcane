# Obby / platformer

The player reads space, chooses a route and commits to timing or precision. Reliable controls/collision and a visible landing matter before stage count. Preserve the actual avatar, movement settings and requested difficulty; generic maximum jump distances are only calibration hypotheses.

## Teach, vary and combine

Prove one short sequence with a safe observation point and meaningful checkpoint. Extend a full-game request through distinct movement situations, locations and combinations rather than recolored pads. Place breathers and checkpoints according to retry cost. Branches, shortcuts or time trials are useful only when they serve the brief; do not invent a compulsory course count.

Own ordered progress and respawn on the server, bind callbacks to the current character/course epoch and distinguish ordinary replay from a new reward. Moving platforms need actual rider testing under latency/frame variation. Geometry must not hide hazards behind decoration or force the camera into a wall. Requested saves load safely; unasked skip products stay absent, and a receipt stub is not a finished purchase feature.

## Presentation and proof

Give each region a coherent silhouette/color/material language that still makes safe surfaces, hazards and the next goal readable. Align mover animation, checkpoint sound, landing feedback, stage HUD and finish/retry flow with real state. Use symbols/text as well as color, device-appropriate controls and valid media assets. `arcane-obby-kit` holds implementation context; gameplay-feel and input-camera guidance support tuning.

Play early, middle and final challenges, an alternate route if delivered, death/retry and full reset. Test duplicate/out-of-order checkpoint touches, death during a mover cycle and returning progress. Record completion/retry friction and separate intentional challenge from inconsistent collision. A flat walkway attribute check proves checkpoint plumbing only, not actual jumping, stage variety or a complete course.
