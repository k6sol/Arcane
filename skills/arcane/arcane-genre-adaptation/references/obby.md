# Obby / platformer

## Player decision
Choose a route and commit to a movement timing or precision challenge.

## Craft priorities
Reliable controls, clear collision, camera visibility and checkpoints matched to difficulty.

## Representative vertical slice
A short sequence that teaches, combines and varies one movement mechanic.

## Player and technical checks
Measure completion/retry friction; distinguish intentional difficulty from inconsistent collisions.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
