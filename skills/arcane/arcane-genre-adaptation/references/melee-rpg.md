# Melee action / RPG

The player reads an opponent, commits to an action and manages position/resources. Choose the requested pace: deliberate dodges/parries, quick combos, cooperative abilities or exploration-led combat. A large damage number or long combo list is not a substitute for readable commitment and counterplay.

## Encounter and progression structure

Prove one encounter with meaningful attack/defense choices and a recovery path. In a full game, vary enemy tells, spaces, group composition, equipment decisions and narrative consequences. New content should change spacing, timing or priorities, not merely repeat a wolf with more HP. A noncombat RPG should not gain mandatory melee just because it uses this profile's progression advice.

Bind windup/active/recovery, actual exported animation markers and server hit windows. Validate actor state, equipped item, resources, range, target and duplicate hit identity. Effects and prediction reconcile to authoritative outcomes. Enemy navigation/leash, death, loot credit and requested persistent rewards must integrate with inventory/quest state; neither an animation playing nor a local 'Done' flag proves a durable grant.

## Character craft and proof

Use actual rigs with clear action silhouettes and contact reactions. Telegraph danger through pose, readable sound and restrained effects rather than unavoidable flashes. HUD resources/cooldowns and equipment comparison should explain trade-offs without covering the encounter. `arcane-combat`, `arcane-npc`, `arcane-inventory` and `arcane-rpg-kit` cover the deeper state/quest path. Verify real media availability and keep unsupported export/import limits explicit.

Test whiffs, interruptions, repeated hits, dodging into obstacles, simultaneous targets, death during reward and the next encounter after retry. Compare different builds in their intended situations and observe whether players can explain why they lost. Requested save/rejoin and multiplayer credit need actual evidence. A training dummy and an XP bar are an early slice, not a full action RPG or proof of satisfying combat.
