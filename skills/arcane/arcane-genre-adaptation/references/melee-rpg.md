# Melee action / RPG

## Player decision
Read an opponent, choose a commitment and manage position/resources.

## Craft priorities
Clear attack windows, counterplay, character builds with trade-offs and animation/state agreement.

## Representative vertical slice
One encounter with two action choices and one non-cosmetic progression decision.

## Player and technical checks
Inspect whiffs, interruption, repeated hits and whether different builds change decisions.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
