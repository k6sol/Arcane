# Adventure / exploration

## Player decision
Choose a destination or tool approach, discover useful information and act on it.

## Craft priorities
Landmarks, traversal purpose, authored surprises and optional routes with consequences.

## Representative vertical slice
A small explorable area, one tool, one discovery and two approach paths.

## Player and technical checks
Check navigation without developer guidance, reward clarity and streaming/rejoin recovery.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
