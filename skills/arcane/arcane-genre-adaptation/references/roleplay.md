# Roleplay / social world

The player chooses an identity/role and creates shared situations with others. Useful places and actions make a world playable: a cafe can take/serve an order, a transit role can carry passengers, a home can be furnished. Do not substitute a compulsory quest grind for self-directed roleplay unless requested.

## Roles connected by activities

Prove one complete role activity and a cooperative interaction in a small connected space. A full request needs the promised usable locations, role tools, customization and social loops, not facades around an empty road. Different roles should exchange something meaningful or create situations for others. Use a content plan proportional to the brief and preserve a small role/door/UI edit's scope.

Server-owned permissions govern role claims, shared objects, edits and activity outcomes. Define role capacity, owner departure, handoff, unavailable tools and recovery. Avoid one client forging another player's job, money or identity. Player-authored text/content follows current platform filtering, audience and safety mechanisms; one filtered response is not universal approval. Private/public spaces and invitations need explicit visibility/consent. Requested saved creations/roles preserve actual ownership and failed-load safety.

## A world that supports expression

Use coherent original architecture, interiors, equipment and navigation landmarks at player scale. Doors, seats, service counters and role props should actually function through supported tools/game code. Character animation, contextual sound and restrained effects make interactions legible without overwhelming conversation. UI presents role purpose, availability, settings and safe social actions; support target devices and avoid intrusive progression pressure. Use authorized assets and no Toolbox by default.

Observe emergent activities with actual participants rather than forcing everyone through a scripted win condition. Test role claims/handoffs, denied edits, shared tool use, filtered content, blocked/expired invites, disconnect and requested rejoin. Review every delivered location for a useful action and safe movement. `arcane-social-moderation`, onboarding, inventory and world-building compose the system. A beautiful town render cannot prove social depth, functioning roles or safe multiplayer behavior.
