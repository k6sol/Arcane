# Roleplay / social world

## Player decision
Choose a role and create a shared situation with other players.

## Craft priorities
Useful places/actions, expressive tools, safe communication and contextual world depth.

## Representative vertical slice
One role loop, a small connected space and one cooperative interaction.

## Player and technical checks
Observe emergent use rather than forced quest completion; test role permissions and content filtering.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
