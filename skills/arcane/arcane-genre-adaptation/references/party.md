# Party / round-based competition

## Player decision
Learn a distinct rule quickly, react or collaborate, then retry with variation.

## Craft priorities
Short teachable goals, different interaction verbs and entertaining eliminated-player flow.

## Representative vertical slice
Two small games with truly different actions and a complete round transition.

## Player and technical checks
Observe whether players can explain each rule; check simultaneous finishes, ties and late joins.

Preserve the user's actual brief and audience. A hybrid uses only the parts that serve its primary loop. Keep monetization optional and separate. Block out and test this slice before scaling content; neither technical correctness nor this profile proves that the game will be popular.
