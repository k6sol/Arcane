# Review the deliverable, not its promise

## Establish the evidence
Compare the original brief and completion register with the actual source, generated assets, imports, player interactions and inspected views. Record the version/project and what was genuinely observed. Keep source implementation, queued/completed jobs, engine placement, persistent uploads, human approval and verified gameplay distinct. A receipt count, model name or ready badge does not prove that a finished asset was imported.

For a full-game request, inspect the complete player journey and planned content set. A single polished slice is an intermediate result. For a scene/asset/feature request, review that actual scope rather than demanding an unrelated whole game. Choose criteria that fit the style; a coherent stylized game is not failed realism, and primitive blockout art is not a finished realistic brief.

## Review independent quality axes
- Function: controls, objectives, rules, rewards, state transitions, progression where required, failure/retry, death/rejoin and cleanup all work through real input.
- Content: the requested spaces, challenges, characters and systems exist and provide meaningful variation. Renamed duplicates and empty menu buttons do not count as breadth.
- Models and surfaces: genuine requested authoring/import provenance, silhouette, joints/attachments, normals, UVs, PBR scale, contact, collisions and variation. Inspect bare structure as well as foliage/texture; don't let a canopy conceal disconnected branches or a render conceal missing imported meshes.
- Scene: finished ground/cover, integrated landmarks/structures, navigable paths, readable composition and lighting. Reject a bare checker baseplate with scattered trees as a completed forest unless that exact blockout was requested.
- Presentation and feel: actual animation transitions, relevant audible/visual feedback, camera response and usable GUI across pending/denied/success/failure states. Empty audio IDs, disconnected event hooks and static screenshots are not integrated feedback.
- Usability and performance: first-time comprehension, accessible input/readability/motion, representative devices/player counts, frame/memory/network budgets and clean lifecycle behavior.

A pass in one axis cannot hide a blocker in another. Numerical root alpha and zero geometric gap cannot overrule an apparently disconnected stalk in an actual view; isolate overlap/framing/material causes and reinspect it. Capture useful player-scale and close-up views, actual motion and audio where relevant. Do not hide defects with fog, bloom, distant framing or arbitrary scores.

## Repair and report honestly
Prioritize inability to play, lost state, unsafe authority, broken imports and misleading completion; then readability, coherence and polish. Make the smallest evidence-backed correction and repeat the original failing path within the actual repair allowance. Preserve manual work and exact approval/receipt boundaries. Unknown dispatch or cleanup is not permission to replay effects.

An ended group with unresolved failures or unverified required work is incomplete. Say which stage failed, what remains and which evidence is missing; do not blame a user approval choice without evidence. Automated checks measure their actual properties, not artistic excellence, exceptional fun or market success. Use real player/human judgment where available and state its limits. Close a requirement only after its stated evidence passes, not because a suggestion or plan sounds comprehensive.
