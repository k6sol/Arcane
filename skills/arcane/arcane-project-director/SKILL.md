# Deliver the requested experience

## Establish the finish line
Classify the request as a complete game, a feature, a scene or an asset. A full-game request requires a complete player journey and an appropriate body of content; a first playable slice is an internal milestone, not the final delivery. Preserve explicit limits, existing work, target devices and the intended style. A small forest still needs its ground, cover, landmark integration and composition; it does not need an unrelated economy.

Keep a compact production contract: player fantasy, core actions, session/social format, beginning/end/retry, content breadth, art direction, required systems, performance targets and observable acceptance. Use `arcane-game-design` and the matching genre skill. If the brief leaves a reversible detail open, choose coherently and proceed; ask only about a consequential unresolved dependency.

## Make a connected production plan
Track each deliverable's owner, dependency, output, integration location and verification. Maintain an asset/content register: source, variants, scale/pivot/collision, material/UV requirements, placement, animation hooks and actual import state. Maps, encounters and challenges need different decisions and pacing, not only renamed copies.

For a whole game, schedule these phases and load their specialist guidance progressively:
- Rules and systems: `arcane-gameplay-systems`, the genre, input/camera, networking, security and persistence where relevant.
- Play spaces and content: level design, world building, original Blender assets, materials and optimization.
- Player presentation: interface, onboarding, animation/gameplay feel, VFX and audio. Decide which feedback each important action and state needs; missing capability is unfinished work, not a silent omission.
- Integration and acceptance: asset integration, playtest QA, performance, accessibility and quality review.

Use bounded independent workstreams when available, with clear outputs and one owner for conflicting Studio writes. Systems and asset creation can proceed concurrently after shared dimensions/state contracts are fixed. Apply dependent changes in order. Do not confuse proposal workers with workers that actually author assets or integrate code.

## Prove the risky path, then finish the scope
First prove one representative input→rule→feedback→outcome→retry path and one real source→export→review→import path. Then complete the requested systems and content matrix. Do not stop at the prototype or spend the whole task polishing one prop while the game cannot be played.

Original models go through real Blender authoring, UV/material/PBR work, preview and verified import. Use available faithful recipes or native authoring; no silent primitive/stock/Toolbox substitute when original finished assets are required. A pending or failed import is not placement. Keep review/upload permissions and receipts exact. If a required capability is missing, record the specific dependency and continue independent authorized work without claiming that dependency complete.

## Completion review
Walk a fresh player's start, learning, main loop, failure, recovery, progression/end and another session where applicable. Check actual scenes, UI states, motion, audio, models and performance together. Named functions, event hooks, queued jobs or screenshots alone are not functioning systems. Resolve critical failures before the success summary. Report implemented, actually verified and pending work with source/job/import/test evidence; do not infer approval or exceptional fun from your own plan.
