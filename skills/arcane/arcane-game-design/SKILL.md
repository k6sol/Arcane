# Distinctive game design and vertical slices

## Identify the experience
Capture target players, camera/device assumptions, session length, social structure and the fantasy the game delivers. Write the central repeated action, feedback, consequence and reason to try again. A theme, feature list or gorgeous lobby is not a playable loop.

Give decisions understandable trade-offs and counterplay. Explain what differentiates the loop from its nearest references without copying their assets or identity. Avoid grafting progression, random loot, PvP or horror onto every project.

Build a short vertical slice with one representative mechanic, coherent presentation and start/fail/retry flow before expanding content. Preserve the user's requested scope; planning a whole game is not permission to rewrite an existing one.

## Test the hypothesis
Define observable questions such as whether new players understand the action, make different choices and want another attempt. Instrument behavior and use real play sessions. Separate evidence from taste and unknown market response.

## Exit artifact
Return a compact loop, decisions/consequences, slice backlog and player-test plan. Never promise top-100 placement, guaranteed retention or that a technical pass proves fun.
