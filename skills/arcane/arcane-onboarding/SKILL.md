# First-session onboarding

## Teach the actual play
Identify the first meaningful action and the minimum knowledge required. Prefer contextual prompts and feedback through the real mechanic over a long slideshow or an unrelated tutorial minigame. The right cadence depends on audience and genre, not a fixed universal number of seconds.

Give a visible immediate goal, explain consequences and make failure/retry understandable. Avoid blocking familiar players or consuming control with excessive popups. Include supported device prompts and accessibility cues.

Track completion separately from merely showing a screen. Save only what the project needs; an interrupted load must not reset a valid profile. Returning players and mid-round joins need an intentional path.

## Verification
Observe first-time players completing the loop without developer narration. Inspect confusing moments, accidental exits, repeated instructions and skip/rejoin behavior. Pair action/funnel evidence with feedback. A completed tutorial flag does not prove understanding or likely retention.
