# Arcane delivery

Read the vault Home, Current State and Next Session, then choose the highest-impact unblocked goal. Verify current remote/source and installed identity where relevant; do not treat a stale handoff as authoritative.

Define the customer-visible outcome and proof before editing. Follow the existing implementation unless evidence warrants a replacement. Keep implementation, controlled fixtures and actual customer acceptance separate. Preserve unrelated work; coordinate path ownership when parallel work is authorized.

Deliver a concrete useful change, inspect relevant validation and publish an SSH-signed concise feature commit under existing authorization. Keep original limits and evidence protections; a passing easier substitute does not satisfy the goal.

Update the goal's acceptance checklist, evidence and next action. Check the whole goal off only after all criteria pass. Keep public commercial dependencies separate from usable-local milestones without removing them from full scope.
